# First public release decisions — prepared for owner review

21 October 2026. These proposals are not approved terms, counsel clearance,
SignPath acceptance or publication authorization. Technical readiness must be
verified independently against the exact installer bytes.

## Prepared release scope

- Product candidate: Integral One 0.1.0, Apache-2.0 with inherited notices and
  dependency exceptions retained. No historical copyright grant is revoked.
- Initial installers: macOS Apple Silicon and Intel; Windows remains a separate
  signed-platform qualification. No Linux/Windows ARM64 readiness claim.
- Mac auto-updates notify first, require download and restart approvals, and
  preserve local identities/data paths. Automatic checks are opt-in. Two-version
  native updater qualification is required before the first release.
- Required signup methods, per owner direction: verified email code, Google and
  Microsoft. Do not publish while either selected social provider is unconfigured
  or its real packaged callback remains untested.
- Signup uses the existing Integral Clerk application's production directory.
  Local owner/workspace identity remains separate from central account identity
  and Business membership. There is no separate registration-server launch.
- Registered accounts are adoption counts, not active use or completed One installs.
  No marketing enrollment, workspace-content upload or activity heartbeat.

## Proposed account and privacy operation

V75 Inc. is the account operator; support@v75inc.com is the approved contact.
Keep a hosted account while its user retains it; do not invent an inactivity purge.
Provide authenticated account access/correction/deletion through the Clerk account
portal and a private support route. Before promising a response deadline, assign
an operator and approve it. Record the actual Clerk processor terms, data locations,
security-log retention and backup/deletion limitations from the selected service
arrangements. Do not claim a retention period the provider has not confirmed.

For a support deletion request, authenticate control of the relevant account and
record the user's scope, canonical issuer/subject and confirmation in a restricted
operator case. Never use a public GitHub issue, plaintext password or chat token
as proof. Delete only the online account the requester owns, using the approved
provider flow and required final confirmation. Verify the online result, record
minimal completion evidence and explain any documented backup/legal exceptions.
Do not remotely delete the local workspace or revoke its durable offline setup.
A support drill and actual operator assignment remain required before launch.

Keep workspace records and model keys local. Disclose separately that a hosted AI
request sends its required context to the selected provider. Publish the reviewed
privacy notice with downloads and account pages before inviting public signups.

2. Approve brand use and the intended launch audience/markets. Domain ownership
   does not establish trademark clearance or exclusivity.
3. Approve the actual privacy/operator/processor arrangements, deletion handling
   and retention disclosures after their configuration is verified. No invented
   age, jurisdiction, hosting-region or retention claims are included here.
4. Record component/native/font/model review and any remaining dependency-risk
   disposition against actual packaged contents. Passing tests is separate.
5. Review the exact source and matching Mac artifacts, signing/notarization and
   clean-install/upgrade/recovery evidence before release publication.
